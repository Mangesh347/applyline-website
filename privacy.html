Privacy policy

Last updated 28 September 2026 · Applyline by Fenwick Labs

What Applyline stores

Your profile, resume, saved answers, job tracker, settings, and (if you add one) your Anthropic API key. All of it is stored with chrome.storage.local on your computer. Fenwick Labs has no servers for Applyline and never receives this data.

What leaves your computer

  • Job applications you fill. Applyline enters your details into application forms on the job sites you use. Those sites receive what you (or Applyline on your behalf) submit.
  • AI tailoring (optional). If you add your own Anthropic API key, the job description and your master resume are sent to api.anthropic.com to score your fit and write a tailored resume and cover letter. This is governed by Anthropic's terms for your API account.

What Applyline never does

  • Sell, share or upload your data to Fenwick Labs or third parties.
  • Fill passwords, government IDs, bank or card details, or dates of birth.
  • Run on sites other than the supported job application platforms listed in the Chrome Web Store.

Permissions

  • storage — keep your profile and tracker on this computer.
  • alarms — start overnight runs at the time you choose.
  • power — keep the computer awake only while an overnight run is active.
  • Job-site access (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, SmartRecruiters, Workday) — read job descriptions and fill application forms.
  • api.anthropic.com — only used when you add your own API key.

Deleting your data

Open Applyline → Settings → Delete all data, or uninstall the extension. Both remove everything Applyline stored.
